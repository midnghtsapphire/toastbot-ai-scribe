# Brand Guidelines

## Brand promise

ToastBot AI Scribe helps people deliver meaningful toasts with confidence.

## Voice and tone

- Encouraging
- Warm and celebratory
- Practical and concise

## Visual direction

- Friendly, high-contrast layouts
- Celebration-forward iconography and copy
- Accessible typography and spacing

## UX principles

- Keep generation steps simple and clear
- Preserve editability and user control of output
- Make rehearsal workflows available in one click
